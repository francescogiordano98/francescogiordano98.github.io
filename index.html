Francesco Giordano

Hi, welcome to my website! I am a third-year PhD student in the Department of Economics and Decision Sciences at HEC Paris.

My research intersects game theory and information economics, with applications to entrepreneurship.

I am advised by Tristan Tomala and Frédéric Koessler.

I am one of the organizers of the Junior Game Theory Seminar at the Institut Henri Poincaré (Paris) and of the HEC PhD Conference. I am also a Research Fellow at the ION Management Science Lab at HEC Paris.
