Francesco Giordano

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Economics and Decision Sciences at HEC Paris.

I study how information and uncertainty shape strategic behavior. My work lies at the intersection of game theory, information design, and decision theory. On the applied side, I study cognitive uncertainty in expert judgment, using data from a leading European startup incubator.

I am one of the organizers of the Junior Game Theory Seminar at the Institut Henri Poincaré, Paris. I am also a research affiliate of the ION Management Science Lab at HEC Paris.

I am advised by Tristan Tomala and Frédéric Koessler.
